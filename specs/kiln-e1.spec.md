# `kiln-e1`

`crates/kiln-e1` owns E1's record contract, coverage arithmetic, evidence gate
and verdict. This file owns the observable behavior of that crate. It cites the
frozen protocol rather than restating it: `docs/experiments.md` E1 ("Footprint
categories", "Gating numerator and verdict", "Audits") and
`experiments/e1-classification-coverage/inputs.md` §1, §3 and §4. A test claims
a criterion by living in the module named for it: `mod kiln_e1_03 { #[test] fn
stub_without_evidence_is_rejected() {...} }` claims `KILN-E1-03`. One criterion
per module; several tests may share it.

## Records and evidence gate

- [KILN-E1-01] A classified unit is read from a JSON line with its subject, position, unit rule (S2, S3 or S5), parameters, absorbed sites and classification; the subject is either a manifest application id or one of TPC-C's five transaction types, and any other subject or unit rule is rejected.
- [KILN-E1-02] A unit labelled static, one-hop or domain-bounded without evidence (no IR node path, or a blank one) is rejected, so an all-static stub carries no verdict.
- [KILN-E1-03] A unit labelled unknown carries its reason and is rejected without one; it may also carry evidence, which must not be blank. A refusal reason on a unit labelled static, one-hop or domain-bounded is rejected as contradictory.
- [KILN-E1-04] An S4 exclusion is accepted only with affirmative evidence: a resolved receiver type that is not `Any`, the called method, and the reason that method has no path to an ORM operation. A missing or blank part, or an `Any` receiver, is rejected, as is an exclusion that also carries a category.
- [KILN-E1-05] A malformed JSONL line fails loudly naming the source and the 1-based line number, and no units are returned.

## Manifest

- [KILN-E1-06] The frozen set is the `owner_repo` of every row of `corpus-manifest.csv` (110 applications); a row with the wrong number of fields, a quoted field, an empty or duplicate id, or a header without `owner_repo` is rejected naming its line.

## Coverage arithmetic

- [KILN-E1-07] An application's share is its units labelled static or one-hop over all its units; domain-bounded and unknown units count in the denominator only, and the domain-bounded share is reported separately.
- [KILN-E1-08] A refused unit counts as unknown and stays in the denominator. Example: 3 static units and 1 refused unit give 75 %.
- [KILN-E1-09] A unit with an S4 exclusion leaves the denominator, and the application reports how many it excluded.
- [KILN-E1-10] The median over the frozen set is the middle share for an odd count and the mean of the two middle shares for an even count, computed exactly: a mean that is exactly 70 % or 50 % is exactly that.
- [KILN-E1-11] TPC-C's share weights each transaction type's share by the frozen mix (New-Order 45 %, Payment 43 %, Order-Status 4 %, Delivery 4 %, Stock-Level 4 %). Example: only Payment fully static and the rest not gives 43 %. The weighting is exact: transaction shares of 100 %, 7/15, 14/15, 0 and 3/10 weigh to exactly 70 %.

- [KILN-E1-17] Shares are exact fractions: they compare without rounding, and a share or sum that does not fit 128 bits makes the evaluation refuse instead of rounding; a share is shown in percent to four decimals.

## Verdict

- [KILN-E1-12] The verdict passes when both shares are at least 70 %, fails when either share is below 50 %, and is otherwise inconclusive; 70 and 50 themselves are not below their threshold, and 69.99 and 49.99 are below it, also when TPC-C's share or the median reaches 70 or 50 exactly only through its fractions. A fail outranks a pass condition on the other share.

## Refusals

- [KILN-E1-13] A manifest application with no unit left in its denominator makes the evaluation refuse and name it; it is never dropped and never counted as 0 %.
- [KILN-E1-14] A classified unit naming an application outside the manifest makes the evaluation refuse and name it.
- [KILN-E1-15] A unit given as TPC-C's that names an application, a unit given as an application's that names a TPC-C transaction type, or a TPC-C transaction type with no unit left in its denominator, makes the evaluation refuse and name it.

## Entry point

- [KILN-E1-16] `kiln-e1 verdict --manifest M --tpcc T --applications A` prints each application's share, TPC-C's weighted share, the median labelled "unweighted — no trace source exists", and the verdict, then exits 0; a refusal or an invalid input prints the reason to stderr and exits non-zero.

## Footprint classifier

The classifier assigns a transaction function of a `kiln-ir` program its footprint category under `docs/experiments.md` ("Footprint categories") and `inputs.md` §5 (Q1-Q4). A resolving read is a read whose result determines an access target of the function: a key, a range bound, or a key to insert. A function is classified by its most demanding feasible path.

- [KILN-E1-18] A function whose every access, over every feasible branch, has every key component, every range prefix and interval bound and every row bound a pure function of its arguments and literals is static, with the IR node path of every access of every branch and of every branch's terminal as evidence. A key column of a point-read row is the operand that read used for that component, so a key built from it is judged by that operand (`inputs.md` §5, Clarification rule 1). A function that touches nothing is static.
- [KILN-E1-19] A branch composes by its arms: the function is classified as the most demanding of its feasible paths, and a continuation that contradicts a presence test already established on its path is not feasible. A branch decided by a read is data-selected, and the read decides either which targets are accessed or only whether an access happens. When two feasible paths that differ in that read's outcome, with every other selector the two paths test held equal, reach target sets that are incomparable (each reaches an access target, a table, key or range, that the other lacks), the read decides which: the function is unknown and names the selecting read. When one path's targets include the other's, the read decides only whether: it is no resolution and makes nothing unknown, the function takes the larger set, and each path still classifies on its own, so a resolving read on either path counts and the larger set never erases it. The comparison is per path, never of the union of an arm's targets, so independent selectors whose arms swap targets are unknown. Two paths' targets are compared up to one consistent renaming of the rows their keys and bounds project from, each path's rows numbered in the order its targets first name them: which row feeds which component of which target counts, and paths whose rows are first named in a different order count as reaching different targets, erring towards unknown. The selecting read identifies no index or foreign key, so it is never a resolution and never earns one-hop. A branch whose paths reach the same targets leaves a static function static, and an arm that holds a resolving read makes the function at least one-hop (Q1.3, Q3.1, Q3.2).
- [KILN-E1-20] A read whose result only reaches the returned value or a written column, and no access target, is no resolving read (Q3.2).
- [KILN-E1-21] A function with exactly one resolving read on its most demanding path is one-hop when that read's result reaches the later access through a declared foreign key (a key projecting, component by component in order, the columns of that foreign key from that read's row: `KILN-E1-40`); the evidence names the read and each access it resolves, and every other access of every branch. One resolving result may determine any number of later accesses, also in a loop over its rows, without adding a hop (Q3.1, Q3.4).
- [KILN-E1-22] A resolving read whose result reaches a later access through a column with no declared foreign key into the accessed table, or through a key column of another table's row that no declared foreign key identifies (where `KILN-E1-40` identifies the foreign key, the navigation is one hop; navigation it does not identify stays unknown), or into the bound of a primary-key range, makes the function unknown with that reason (Q2.5, `docs/experiments.md` "Unknown"). A key of an accessed table with a component that is arithmetic over a read result is no read's column or key: it keeps neither foreign-key identity nor range containment, so it is unknown with that reason. A key of several components with a read-derived component is unknown unless `KILN-E1-37` or `KILN-E1-40` holds, and the reason names the table.
- [KILN-E1-23] A key to insert derived from a read is that function's one resolution, whichever column it projects; a second resolving read still exceeds one (Q3.5).
- [KILN-E1-24] A range over a declared index whose bounds are pure functions of arguments is static; resolving its rows into base-row accesses of the index's table, or through a foreign key, is one hop when it is the only resolving read. The evidence names, for each index range read of every branch, which applies: index-only, feeding another index range's bounds, or followed by base-row accesses; a read that only decides a branch is index-only. A range over an index the schema does not declare is unknown, and a primary-key range is never taken for an index (Q2.1-Q2.5).
- [KILN-E1-25] Two or more resolving reads exceed one hop whether they are independent or chained, so such a function is never one-hop; the unknown reason says which (Q3.3, Q3.7, Q3.8).
- [KILN-E1-26] A function whose two or more resolving reads are chained is domain-bounded only when it is provably confined to one partition the schema declares (Q3.7). kiln-ir declares no partition, so such a function is unknown with the reason "no declared partition", whatever its arguments look like, and the classifier never labels a function domain-bounded.
- [KILN-E1-27] Every proven label carries non-blank evidence and every unknown label its reason, so the result is a classification the evidence gate (`KILN-E1-02`, `KILN-E1-03`) accepts. A program that `Program::validate` rejects, including a key component that can never fit its key column (`KILN-IR-82`), is reported as invalid and not classified. A name the program does not declare as a function, a read of a slot no earlier read bound, and an iteration over a slot an ended iteration released are reported instead of classified. A slot released by the end of the iteration that bound it holds no row, as the evaluator rebinds it (`KILN-IR-15`), so a presence test on it takes only its absent arm. A projection finds no row in such a slot, in a slot a range read bound (a range's rows are current only inside an iteration over them), or in a slot whose point read a presence test on the path found absent; every place the evaluator reads a slot aborts on such a projection, and so does a key component projecting a column whose declared kind its key column cannot take from a slot whose table the path knows, also in a primary-key range's prefix or interval bounds (`KILN-IR-82` rejects it up front only where every table the slot can hold misfits), so the path ends there with every access before it and none after. The aborting place keeps an access of its own only where the evaluator logs it first: an insert or update logs its write before it resolves its written columns. The classifier walks an iteration's body once, as its first iteration. A body that aborts on that first iteration aborts only when the range holds a row, so the path forks on that range read's presence, compared like a presence test on it (`KILN-E1-19`), and the path where the range holds no row skips the body. A slot the body reads before binding it within the iteration, at any place the evaluator reads a slot, while the body also binds or releases it at any depth (a read into it, or an inner iteration that takes it as its row or binds it) is carried: a later iteration reads what an earlier one left there, which one walk does not see, so the function is unknown with the reason that the slot is carried across iterations, whatever the first iteration does, and the evidence is the node of that use. The iteration's own row slot is never carried, since each iteration binds it afresh.
- [KILN-E1-28] A branch on a comparison whose operands involve no read result (arguments and literals only) is argument-selected: it composes by its arms like any branch, each arm's path classifying on its own, so it is static when each arm is static and never unknown for selecting different targets (Q1). Its outcomes are constraints on its paths, excluded from data-selector candidacy: the pairwise data-selection check (`KILN-E1-19`) compares only paths that agree on every argument decision, so a presence test inside the arms never pairs paths from different argument arms. It records no selector, and it does not make a read a resolving read.
- [KILN-E1-29] A branch on a comparison with an operand that projects a read result, alone or mixed with arguments, is data-selected by the branch node: paths that differ only in its outcome and reach incomparable target sets make the function unknown naming the branch's terminal node as selector, under the same per-path rule as a presence test (`KILN-E1-19`); arms whose target sets are ordered by inclusion, or equal, leave a static function static (the comparison decides only whether).
- [KILN-E1-37] A key of a primary-key range's table whose every component projects the matching key column of a row that range bound is covered by the range and adds no resolution; the same key over a declared index range of that table is one resolution by the index read. A key whose components are not all so matched, with a read-derived component and no declared foreign key it matches (`KILN-E1-40`), is unknown naming the table, also when its other components are static (`inputs.md` §5, Clarification rules 2 and 4).

## TPC-C lowering and classified output

The TPC-C units are lowered by hand from Clauses 1.3 and 2.4-2.8 of TPC-C Revision 5.11 (`inputs.md` §1), under the lowering rules of `inputs.md` §5 (Q1, Q2, Q4). A step the IR cannot express refuses the whole function, and a refused function is an unknown unit (Q4.1). The committed data holds categories with evidence and reasons only, never a coverage figure or a verdict.

- [KILN-E1-30] The TPC-C schema is a valid `kiln-ir` schema with the nine tables of Clause 1.3.1. Every table declares its primary key in Clause 1.3.1's column order among its columns (HISTORY has none and is keyless), and no index: the specification declares none, so none is added. Its foreign keys are `KILN-E1-41`.
- [KILN-E1-31] Each of the five transactions is lowered from its profile (2.4.2.2, 2.5.2.2, 2.6.2.2, 2.7.4.2, 2.8.2.2) with every feasible branch, Payment's and Order-Status's by-name paths included, or refused in full. A refusal names every step the IR cannot express, each with its clause and why, and never drops an effect, a failure or a response.
- [KILN-E1-32] A lookup of CUSTOMER by (C_W_ID, C_D_ID, C_LAST) (Payment, 2.5.2.2; Order-Status, 2.6.2.2) and of ORDER by (O_W_ID, O_D_ID, O_C_ID) (Order-Status, 2.6.2.2) is a secondary-attribute lookup with no declared index. It blocks the lowering with that reason and never gets an invented index (Q2.5).
- [KILN-E1-33] A step that inserts into a table that has no primary key (HISTORY, Clause 1.3.1; it needs an append that keeps duplicates) blocks the lowering: `kiln-ir` inserts only at a key. A step that keys a row by several columns or must enforce a foreign key of several columns also refuses, though `kiln-ir` expresses both: the lowering is not redone onto them yet and waits on the capabilities of #61 that have not landed (prefix ranges, keyless append, scans and selection, collection processing, text and formatting, structured responses, the host boundary), which its refusal reason says. No key is encoded and none is invented. Time, OL_QUANTITY * I_PRICE and New-Order's bounded sum are not blockers: the engine supplies time as an argument and the rest is argument-selected repeated addition. New-Order's rollback response with the allocated O_ID, exact decimal scaling and null encodings are blockers of their own.
- [KILN-E1-34] The by-id-only variants of Payment and Order-Status are lowered or refused the same way and reported apart from the five gating units, so a sensitivity unit never reaches the verdict.
- [KILN-E1-35] A classified unit is written as one JSON line that `kiln-e1 verdict` reads back to the same unit, for a unit labelled static, one-hop, domain-bounded or unknown and for an S4 exclusion; the same units always give the same bytes.
- [KILN-E1-36] `kiln-e1 tpcc --out F --sensitivity-out S` writes F with one unit per transaction type, in the order of the frozen mix, and S with the sensitivity units; F is accepted by the verdict reader and its evidence gate. It rejects identical or aliased `--out` and `--sensitivity-out` paths before writing either. It writes the same bytes on every run and prints no share and no verdict. A missing or unknown flag, or a path that cannot be written, prints the reason to stderr and exits non-zero.
- [KILN-E1-38] A key computed from arguments and literals by multiplication or a conditional value is static; a key computed from a read result by either, in a condition or an arm, is unknown with the reason of `KILN-E1-22`, naming the table.
- [KILN-E1-39] A conditional value is judged by two separate rules. What it is made of is syntactic: its condition and both arms count, reached or not, so a read result anywhere in it makes it computed from a read (`KILN-E1-38`), even when its condition is decided and both arms project the same column; a column of a slot an ended iteration released names no read result. Whether it certainly aborts (`KILN-E1-27`) applies reachability first: a statically decided condition (two literals compared, or a test of a released slot or of a read whose presence the path settled) runs only its chosen arm, so nothing in the other arm aborts. An undecided one certainly aborts when both arms do and never when neither may; otherwise the data decides whether the call goes on, hiding the later accesses from the comparison of `KILN-E1-19`, so the function is unknown with that reason and the evidence is the node holding the conditional. A slot with no row and a key component of a kind its key column cannot take are one cause, whichever arm holds which. A body over a range the path knows is empty never runs, so its conditionals neither abort nor refuse. The carried-slot scan (`KILN-E1-27`) counts a slot read in either arm of a conditional, and in its condition, as a use.
- [KILN-E1-40] A key with a read-derived component is one resolution by read R when a declared foreign key from R's table onto the accessed table has the key's components, in order, as its columns, each component projecting that column of the one row R produced or equal to it by an alias (a key column of a point-read row is the operand the read used for it). A computed component, a component of another row, a component not provably equal to its column, a reordered or partial match is no match: the key is unknown naming the table (`inputs.md` §5, Clarification rule 2 (d), (e) and its two examples).
- [KILN-E1-42] A primary-key range's prefix, interval bounds and row bound are judged component by component like a key. Each fills the key column at its position (the interval bounds the column after the prefix) and passes the certain-abort analysis before any provenance is asked, in the order the evaluator resolves them. A prefix or interval bound from arguments and literals is static, an omitted interval likewise; one derived from a read result, computed or not, makes the function unknown with the reason that the range is bounded by a read result. A slot read in a prefix or interval bound is a use for the carried-slot scan (`KILN-E1-27`).
- [KILN-E1-43] A key column of a row an iteration takes from a primary-key range, covered by a prefix component that reads no result, is that prefix component's operand (`inputs.md` §5, Clarification rule 1); the interval's column and the columns after it stay columns of the row. A key of an accessed table whose components, in order, are the columns of one declared foreign key from the range's table, each the row's column or equal to it by that alias, is one resolution by the range read, and a key of the range's table made of the row's key columns and the prefix operands is covered by the range (`KILN-E1-37`, `KILN-E1-40`). The alias ends with the iteration: the row slot is released and its projections abort (`KILN-E1-27`). A prefix derived from a read gives no alias, and the range is unknown (`KILN-E1-42`).
- [KILN-E1-41] The TPC-C schema declares every foreign key of Clause 1.3.1 over its parent's whole primary key in the parent's key order (district to warehouse; customer to district; history to customer and to district; new-order to order; order to customer; order-line to order and to stock; stock to warehouse and to item), and no unique constraint besides the keys, as the clause declares none. The hand lowering still blocks on a foreign key of several columns until it is regenerated against this schema.
- [KILN-E1-44] A key computed from arguments and literals by concatenation, truncation or a conditional on a containment is static; one computed from a read result by any of them, in an operand, a length or a containment condition, is unknown with the reason of `KILN-E1-22`, naming the table. A branch on a containment is judged like a branch on a comparison (`KILN-E1-28`, `KILN-E1-29`): argument-selected when no operand involves a read result, data-selected otherwise. The certain-abort analysis of `KILN-E1-27` and `KILN-E1-39` covers the slot reads of these forms: a projection in an operand ends the path, a text form aborting on one arm of an undecided conditional makes the function unknown, and a containment of two text literals is a statically decided condition that runs its chosen arm alone. The same analysis ends a path at a projected operand whose table the path knows and whose column cannot be what the form needs (a non-text column as an operand of a concatenation, a truncation's text or a containment, a non-integer column as a truncation length), as the evaluator aborts there, and a text form fed such an operand on one arm of an undecided conditional makes the function unknown. A branch on a containment passes the same analysis before its operands or either continuation are inspected. A form that certainly aborts whatever the path, reached or not, is rejected before classification as an invalid program (`KILN-IR-96`).
- [KILN-E1-45] The carried-slot scan (`KILN-E1-27`) counts a slot read by a concatenation, a truncation (its text or its length) or a containment condition as a use.
